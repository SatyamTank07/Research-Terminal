1. Executive summary

Microsoft compounds on a diversified cloud and productivity franchise. Commercial cloud revenue grew 22% year over year as Azure consumption broadened across enterprise workloads2, and operating margin held at 44.6%1. Our base-case value sits 9.0% above the $378.10 price, inside the ±10% band, so the stance is Fairly Valued.

Bottom line: quality is fully priced; the market-implied growth of 2.4% sits below our base case, leaving modest asymmetry.

Switching costs across the Microsoft 365 installed base and integrated identity, security and data services anchor a durable moat3.

Cash conversion stays above 100% of net income, with stock-based compensation shown as a memo line rather than added back1.
